This marks the first studio post. I plan on using this as a free space to post whatever I'd like, like a blog or a diary for my work and practice - a stream of things I'm doing. This is a picture in my studio from a few months ago of me and my friend Alejandro. I chose this picture for the first post because I am reflecting on why I like painting with people. I like setting up situations in which I am creating with others. do I just like to perform? I do, but I don't think it is only that. I also like the friction and sparks and fires that occur when people are creating around each other. I like when I am alone too thinking too. More on that later. Bye for now.
